Detection Settings

Three fresh readings avoid momentary false alarms.

NO_LOAD · Confirmed ON and below 30% of rated watts (minimum 1 W), for 3 readings.
PHANTOM_LOAD · Confirmed OFF with more than 5 W for 3 readings.
OVERLOAD · Above the configured percentage of rated power for 3 readings.
NO_ACK / STALE_TELEMETRY · No matching relay confirmation after 8 s, or no fresh telemetry for 15 s.
